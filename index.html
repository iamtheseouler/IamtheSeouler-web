Everyday Voyager
Seouler /ˈsoʊlər/ noun

someone who treats their own city like a destination — turning the everyday into discovery, wherever they happen to be rooted.

자기가 사는 도시를 여행지처럼 대하는 사람. 뿌리내린 곳에서, 매일을 발견으로 바꾸는 사람.

Rooted, but restless — in the best way.

The Origin

I met my most alive self
on a trip.

여행에서, 나는 가장 살아있는 나를 만났다.

It was my first time abroad alone — Macau, then Hong Kong. Somewhere in those unfamiliar streets, I found a version of myself I loved: curious, awake, unafraid. Then I came home, and within days she was gone. Back to the same routines, the same quiet dullness.

So I asked myself a question. What if the problem wasn't the trip — but the way I was looking at my own life?

Seoul is on someone's dream list right now. A place people long to visit but can't. And I live here. So I decided to stop waiting for the next trip, and start treating my own city like the destination it already is.

Live where you are, like a Seouler.

당신이 있는 그곳을, 서울러처럼 살아보기를.
